### Aim of the experiment

The experiment aims to provide the user an introduction to the Central Limit Theorem, its proof and how widely this theorem is applicable in real-life scenarios. More specifically, the experiment aims to :-

- State and explain the central limit theorem with a few examples. 
- Provide the intuition and proof of the central limit theorem via characteristic functions and fourier transforms. 
- Develop an understanding of how to use central limit theorem in real life. 
- Help user visualize the CLT for various distributions and develop an inuitive understanding.
